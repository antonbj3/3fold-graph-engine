need soundness, useful width, predictable effort, graph identity, amortized queries →
free global structure is shared across queries while witness slack depends on
terminal geometry and update ordering → decompose U/R and R/L → hub degree does
not specify which factor fails → measure both against exact references, change
flow support and dual potential independently, then remove electrically inactive
leaf support and extend the full original-graph witness exactly.

400 small graphs are instruments for the routing conjecture. Full F1 exact
references and unchanged-source hashes are the real structural stress test.
