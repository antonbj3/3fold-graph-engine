# Prior work behind the modules, and what each source says next to our numbers

Read by agents from primary sources during the September 2026 round; entries marked (unverified) were not confirmed against the
paper's own tables. Numbers in the right column are ours (RESULTS.md); the middle column is the source's result.

| module / question | source and its result | relation to ours |
|---|---|---|
| resistance_sketch | Spielman & Srivastava 2011 (arXiv 0803.0929): Õ(n log n/ε²) sampled edges preserve all quadratic forms; Koutis–Miller–Peng solvers; all-pairs R_eff in m^{1+o(1)}ε^{−1.5} by short-cycle decomposition (Chu et al. 2018, arXiv 1805.12051); dynamic R_eff maintenance Õ(m^{4/5}) (arXiv 1804.04038) | the sketch is the Spielman–Srivastava estimator; dynamic maintenance and all-pairs batching are not used |
| 4-bit codes | RaBitQ (Gao & Long 2024, arXiv 2405.12497): unbiased inner-product estimator with error O(1/√D); Extended RaBitQ (2409.09913); TurboQuant (2504.19874) notes MSE-optimal scalar codes are biased for inner products unless a QJL residual is added | our codes have no inner-product bias correction; measured loss on ranking ≤ 0.008 Spearman, so the bias did not matter at k = 64 |
| graph_interface | Dörfler & Bullo 2013 (arXiv 1102.2950): Kron reduction = Schur complement of the Laplacian on boundary nodes, exact for the boundary response | our two-graph federation is Kron reduction with the harmonic measure as the per-node interface; the platform's own T4 "Schur = universal composition" law says the same |
| probe value ≈ E[Δθ²] | Wootters 1981 (Phys. Rev. D 23:357): statistical distance = Hilbert-space angle; Braunstein & Caves 1994 | no prior work found that uses Fisher–Rao travel as an acquisition function (agents searched; absence unverified) |
| regime_posterior, ≤ 1 transition | Karp & Kleinberg 2007 noisy binary search Θ(ε^{−2} log n); Nowak 2011 generalized binary search (arXiv 0910.4397) near-minimax for threshold learning; Gotovos et al. 2013 level-set estimation with GP bounds | the entropy drop on a fixed partition is generalized binary search; no rate bound is claimed for the noisy default |
| non-myopic design | Foster et al. 2021 DAD (arXiv 2103.02438): amortized non-myopic policies gain ~24 % total EIG over greedy on location finding; Blau et al. 2022 RL-BOED (2202.00821) | ours is one-step; e4 shows the greedy value predicts realized gain exactly; lookahead not tested |
| unlock_value order | Mitten 1960 / Butterworth 1972 series-system sequencing; Garey 1973, Sidney 1975 for chain precedence; Weitzman 1979 Pandora's box index applies to "keep the best box", not to "settle every node" | brute-force optimal in tests for no and chain precedence; Pandora's index is not the right object for series goals |
| plan_value | Chow 1970 selective prediction; classification with costly features; e5/e6 cascades | settle-cost DP per node; e16: cascade 3–5× cheaper at ~1 % error, τ is the knob, DP = "judge first" in the tested range |
| replay_policy features | Science4Cast (Krenn et al., Nat. Mach. Intell. 2023; arXiv 2210.00881): hand-built network features beat end-to-end GNNs on "which concepts connect next"; Poursafaei et al. 2022 EdgeBank (2207.10128): memorization baseline rivals temporal GNNs; Sourati & Evans 2023 (Nat. Hum. Behav.): author–concept hypergraph walks improve discovery prediction up to ~400 % where literature is sparse (unverified magnitude) | our label (first co-citation within 24 months) is their label; author overlap, EdgeBank recency and Uzzi atypicality are the three untested feature families; Gu & Krenn 2024 Impact4Cast (2402.08640) uses an impact label, closer to "worth doing" |
| e3b far pairs → lower impact | Uzzi et al. 2013 (Science 342:468): highest impact = conventional core + one atypical combination; Ke et al. 2015 sleeping beauties | e3b measures the raw pair distance only; the "conventional core + atypical" shape is not tested |
| claim_federation inferred links | context-filtered ABC discovery (PLOS ONE 2019, PMID 31017923): precision 27.1 % → 71.4 % and 22.1 % → 77.9 % when bridges are filtered by context | our planted 0.23 → 0.71 is not validated against this paper; the shape is the same, the numbers are independent |
| lineage_information | Simkin & Roychowdhury 2003 "Read before you cite": ~70–90 % of citations are copied from other reference lists; Dong, Berti-Équille & Srivastava 2009 copy detection: 51–69 % error reduction over independence/voting; Cochrane Handbook ch. 23 unit-of-analysis (never count a shared group twice) | copies collapse to one origin; no measured copying rate on our data |
| source_reliability | Dawid & Skene 1979; TruthFinder (Yin et al. 2008): ~95 % vs ~89.5 % voting; Snorkel label model (Ratner et al. 2019) with dependency structure | EM over roots with a Beta prior; recovered planted rates within 0.07 (test) |
| composition of signed relations | Kuipers QSIM (1986): complete but not sound — composing local signs over-generates; Forbus QPT I+/I− ambiguity when influences conflict | our inferred links are the composed signs; "ambiguous" is not a state we emit yet |
| polarity_rules | MacCartney & Manning 2009 natural logic; MonaLog (Hu et al. 2020, arXiv 1910.08772): 98.5 % precision on entailments, 95 % on contradictions; MonaLog labels train BERT to 87.5 % | rule 0.86 where it answers on QuaRTz; rule labels calibrate the language model to 0.927 = same as true labels (e14, e18) |
| lens_pooling | Zheng et al. 2023 PriDe (2309.03882): estimate the option-ID prior on a subsample, cheaper than permutations; Zhao et al. 2021 Calibrate Before Use (up to +30 points); Zhou et al. 2023 Batch Calibration (label-free) | e18: label-free batch calibration gives +0.01 here — the fault is per-form sign inversion, not option prior; per-form labels are needed and the rule supplies them |
| first-token logits | Wang et al. 2024 (2402.14499): first-token argmax disagrees with generated text in > 60 % of cases in some settings | our fields read logits after a fixed answer format; disagreement with generation not measured |
| typed_extraction speed | SGLang RadixAttention (2312.07104), XGrammar (MLSys 2025): prefix sharing and grammar decoding | e13: prefill once + tiled cache = 9× on our hardware; the open replica of the hosted service does exactly this and has no training |
| calibration form | Niculescu-Mizil & Caruana 2005: Platt fails when score–accuracy saturates; Kull et al. 2017 beta calibration | our Platt failure (0.44 stated / 0.63 right) is that case; isotonic used |
| record_guarantee | Angelopoulos, Bates, Candès, Jordan, Lei 2021/2022 risk-controlling prediction sets, Learn-then-Test | Bonferroni over a fixed grid; two wrong versions caught by the held-out test |
| self-improving search | AlphaEvolve (2506.13131), Darwin Gödel Machine (2505.22954): SWE-bench 20 → 50 % by self-edits scored on a frozen benchmark | our e3c residual growth is the only self-improvement measured; a frozen evaluation suite for the selector is not built |
